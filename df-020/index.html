DF-020 Cybersecurity Basics for Investigators

Five short modules on the computer, network and security basics that every investigator needs, and the traces that attacks leave behind. Taught by Anil Sharma. Learn at your own speed.

5 modules, about 5 minutes of video eachActivities and quizzesNo prior experience neededEnglish, with captions
₹399one-time fee, including GST · certificate of completion included
Join the waitlist, ₹399See what is covered

What you will learn

Work through the modules in order. In each one, watch the video, try the activity, then take the quiz.

Module 1

How computers store and record data

The parts of a computer, what an operating system and a file system do, what delete really means, and where Windows and Linux record activity.

Video · activity · 5-question quiz

Module 2

Networking essentials for investigators

IP and MAC addresses, DNS, ports, what happens when you open a website, and where network traces are kept.

Video · activity · 5-question quiz

Module 3

Security fundamentals

The CIA triad, threat, vulnerability and risk, authentication, and the difference between encryption and hashing.

Video · activity · 5-question quiz

Module 4

Common attacks and the traces they leave

Phishing, malware, ransomware, credential theft, insider misuse and denial of service, the attack chain and indicators of compromise.

Video · activity · 5-question quiz

Module 5

Logs, time and your first response

Log sources and how to read a log line, converting IST and UTC, the four phases of incident response, the first hour and a short case note.

Video · activity · 5-question quiz

Final assessment and certificate

When you have finished all five modules, take the final assessment and submit a one-page case note.

  • The final assessment has 10 questions. The pass mark is 70 percent.
  • You receive a certificate of completion when you pass and your case note is accepted.

About the certificate. It records that you completed a course. It is not an accredited qualification.

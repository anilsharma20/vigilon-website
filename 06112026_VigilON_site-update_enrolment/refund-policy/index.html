Refund policy

When you can get a refund for a VigilON online course, and how to ask for one.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short

Not right for you? Email us within 7 days of payment and we will refund you in full. The one exception: if you have already submitted the final assessment or the case note for that course.

How to ask

Email asharma@vigilon.in with your name, the email address you paid with, and the payment reference from your receipt. Your access will be switched off when we approve the refund.

The refund goes back to the way you paid, and your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 working days. We will email you when we start it.

The Starter bundle

The same rule applies. If a course was not yet released when you paid, your 7 days for it start on the day you get access to it.

If something went wrong

If you paid but did not get your access email within one working day, or you were charged twice, write to us and we will fix it or refund the extra payment.

If you paid through an institute, the institute handles refunds under its own policy. Please contact the institute first.

Changes

We may update this policy. The policy in force on the day you paid applies to your purchase.

Three levels, set by what you handle

Your target level depends on the sensitivity of the Federal Contract Information (FCI) and Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) in your contracts, not on ambition.

1

Foundational

Basic safeguarding of Federal Contract Information (FCI).

Annual self-assessment17 practices
2

Advanced

Protection of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

Self or C3PAO assessment110 practices (NIST SP 800-171)
3

Expert

Highest-risk programs and enhanced CUI protection.

Government-led assessment110 practices plus NIST SP 800-172 enhanced controls

How we get you there

Three engagement types, used on their own or as a full journey from first gap analysis to year-round maintenance.

Gap analysis & SSP build

CMMC-GA

A control-by-control review against your target level, scored with SPRS methodology, with a prioritized POA&M.

  • NIST SP 800-171 / 800-172 control mapping
  • System Security Plan drafting and review
  • CUI boundary and data-flow diagrams

Assessment preparation

CMMC-AP

Mock assessments run the way a C3PAO will run them, so evidence, interviews and artifacts are ready before assessment day.

  • Evidence package organization
  • Mock C3PAO interviews with staff
  • DFARS flow-down review

Managed compliance

CMMC-MC

Ongoing support for teams that need compliance maintained between assessments, not just achieved once.

  • Quarterly control monitoring
  • Policy and SSP version control
  • Subcontractor flow-down tracking

How an engagement runs

Most contractors move through all five stages in four to eight weeks, depending on target level and current maturity.

1

Discovery

Scope the CUI/FCI boundary and target level

2

Gap assessment

Score all controls, draft the POA&M

3

Remediation

Close gaps, write policy and SSP

4

Mock assessment

Rehearse interviews and evidence

5

Certification

Support through the C3PAO assessment

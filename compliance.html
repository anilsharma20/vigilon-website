CMMC

The three levels, plainly

Your target level is set by the sensitivity of the Federal Contract Information (FCI) and Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) you handle — not by ambition.

LEVELSCOPEASSESSED BYPRACTICES
01

Foundational

Basic safeguarding of Federal Contract Information (FCI)
Annual self-assessment
17 practices
02

Advanced

Protection of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)
Self or C3PAO assessment
110 practices — NIST SP 800-171
03

Expert

Highest-risk programs, enhanced CUI protection
Government-led assessment
110 + NIST SP 800-172 enhanced controls
